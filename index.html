Privacy Policy for Gulp Hole

Last updated: October 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Gulp Hole (“the game,” “we,” or “us”) handles information when you play the game, view advertisements, receive notifications, or contact us.

For privacy questions or requests, contact waleediqbale678@gmail.com.

1. Game Progress Stored on Your Device

Gulp Hole saves gameplay information locally on your device, including:

  • Coins, level progress, and earned stars.
  • Unlocked and selected skins.
  • Achievements and gameplay statistics.
  • Endless mode high scores.
  • Daily reward history.
  • Tutorial completion and game preferences.

The current version does not require a player account or upload your progress for cloud synchronization. Your progress does not automatically transfer between devices.

Clearing the game’s storage or uninstalling it removes the local save.

Local saves are separate from the information processed by advertising and notification services described below.

2. Advertising

Gulp Hole displays banner advertisements using Google AdMob.

Google’s advertising services may collect and share information such as IP addresses, advertising or other device identifiers, advertisement interactions, and diagnostic information. An IP address may be used to estimate your approximate location.

This information supports advertising, measurement, service operation, security, and fraud prevention. Ad personalization depends on your choices, applicable requirements, and service configuration.

The current version does not offer rewarded advertisements or interstitial advertisements.

For more information, see Google’s Privacy Policy and Google Mobile Ads data disclosures.

3. Advertising Privacy Choices

Gulp Hole uses Google’s User Messaging Platform to request advertising consent where required.

When Google’s consent system requires an ongoing privacy-options entry point, an Ad privacy choices button is available in the game’s Settings. You can use it to review or change the choices Google makes available.

You can also manage available advertising privacy controls through your Android device settings.

Changing advertising preferences does not necessarily stop all advertising-related processing. Information may still be processed to deliver permitted advertisements, maintain security, or prevent fraud.

4. Push Notifications

Gulp Hole uses OneSignal and Google Firebase Cloud Messaging to support push notifications, such as game reminders and announcements.

Notification services may process push tokens, subscription identifiers, notification-permission status, device and operating-system details, app version, language, time zone, IP address, app-session information, and notification interactions.

This information supports notification delivery, audience management, and delivery or engagement measurement. Some SDK information may be processed when the app starts, even if you do not allow notifications.

You can disable notifications in the game’s Settings or through Android’s notification settings. Disabling notifications stops messages to that subscription; it does not automatically delete previously collected information or disable every SDK function.

See OneSignal’s Privacy Policy and its SDK data collection information.

5. Information You Provide When Contacting Us

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you include. We use this information to respond, investigate reported problems, and handle privacy requests.

Please do not send passwords or unnecessary sensitive information.

6. Service Providers and International Processing

Information may be processed by Google, OneSignal, and their service providers to operate the advertising and notification features described above. These services may process information in countries other than your own.

We may also disclose information where required by law or reasonably necessary to address fraud, security issues, or legal claims.

7. Retention and Deletion

Local gameplay information remains on your device until you clear the game’s storage or uninstall it.

Advertising and notification providers retain information according to their policies, service settings, and applicable obligations. Uninstalling the game does not automatically delete information already held by those providers.

Earlier versions offered accounts and Firebase cloud saves. Removing these features does not automatically delete records created with those versions. To request deletion of an older account or cloud save, email waleediqbale678@gmail.com and identify the account you used. Do not include its password.

We may need reasonable information to verify and locate a deletion request. Some information may need to be retained for legal, security, or fraud-prevention purposes.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, or restrictions on the use of your personal information, or to withdraw consent.

Contact us using the email address above. For information controlled independently by a service provider, its privacy policy explains additional request options.

9. Children’s Privacy

The current version does not ask players to create an account or submit their name, email address, or age to play.

Advertising and notification SDKs may nevertheless process device-related information as described in this policy. If you are a parent or guardian with a concern about a child’s information, contact us so we can investigate and address the request.

10. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect information handled through the game. No method of storage or transmission can guarantee complete security.
