Privacy Policy for Gulp Hole

Last updated: September 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Gulp Hole handles information when you use the game.

By using Gulp Hole, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information Stored on Your Device

Gulp Hole stores certain game information locally on your device so that your progress can be preserved between game sessions.

This may include:

  • Your coin balance
  • Level progress
  • Stars earned on levels
  • Unlocked and selected skins
  • Achievements and gameplay statistics
  • Endless mode high score
  • Daily login streak information
  • Sound and vibration settings
  • Tutorial progress
  • Rewarded advertisement cooldown information

This information is primarily stored locally using Android application storage.

2. Accounts and Firebase Authentication

Gulp Hole may allow players to create or sign into an account using services provided by Google Firebase Authentication.

Depending on the authentication method you choose, Firebase may process information such as:

  • Email address
  • Account authentication information
  • Google account authentication information
  • IP address
  • Device or browser information used for security and authentication

Password authentication is handled by Firebase Authentication. Gulp Hole does not directly receive or store your account password.

3. Cloud Game Saves

If you sign into an account, Gulp Hole may store your game progress using Google Firebase services so that your progress can be restored or synchronized.

Cloud-saved game information may include:

  • Coin balance
  • Level progress
  • Stars
  • Unlocked skins
  • Selected skin
  • Achievements and gameplay statistics
  • Endless mode high score
  • Game settings related to your saved profile

Gulp Hole is designed so that gameplay can also continue using locally stored progress when an internet connection is unavailable. Unsynchronized progress may be uploaded when an internet connection becomes available again.

4. Advertising

Gulp Hole displays advertisements using Google AdMob.

The game may contain:

  • Rewarded advertisements
  • Interstitial advertisements

Rewarded advertisements are optional advertisements that players may choose to watch in exchange for an in-game benefit such as coins or a gameplay revive.

Google Mobile Ads may automatically collect or process certain information for advertising, analytics, security, and fraud prevention purposes. This may include:

  • IP address
  • General location derived from an IP address
  • Advertising or device identifiers
  • App interactions, such as taps and advertisement interactions
  • Diagnostic and performance information
  • Information about advertisements displayed to your device

The way this information is used is governed by Google's privacy policies and advertising policies.

You can learn more about Google's privacy practices here:

Google Privacy Policy

5. Personalized Advertising and Consent

Depending on your location, Google or Gulp Hole may request your consent before using certain information for personalized advertising.

Where required, advertising preferences and consent choices may be presented through Google's consent and advertising systems.

You may also be able to control or reset advertising-related identifiers through your Android device settings.

6. Third-Party Services

Gulp Hole currently uses third-party services that may process information according to their own privacy policies.

These services may include:

  • Google Firebase Authentication
  • Google Firebase / Firestore cloud storage services
  • Google AdMob
  • Google Play services

Relevant privacy information can be found at:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Privacy and Security
  • Google's Advertising Policies and Technologies

7. Children's Privacy

Gulp Hole does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children beyond information that may be processed by the third-party services described in this Privacy Policy.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe that a child has provided personal information through Gulp Hole in a way that should not have occurred, please contact us.

8. Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect game information. Cloud-based information is processed using services provided by Google Firebase.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Data Retention

Local game information may remain stored on your device until the application data is cleared or the application is removed.

Cloud game information associated with an account may remain stored in Firebase while the account and game save remain active.

10. Your Choices

Depending on the features you use, you may have choices including:

  • Playing without using certain account features
  • Choosing whether to watch rewarded advertisements
  • Managing advertising settings through your Android device
  • Signing out of your Gulp Hole account
  • Clearing locally stored application data through Android settings

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Gulp Hole adds, removes, or changes features, services, advertising systems, or data practices.

Any updated Privacy Policy will be published on this page and the "Last updated" date will be changed accordingly.
